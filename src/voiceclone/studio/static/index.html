    Tips for a good sample
    • 15 to 30 seconds is best. The limits are 5 and 45 seconds.
    • One person only, with no other voices and no music.
    • Natural speech, at a steady volume.
    • Noise is fine: the studio removes it. Strong echo is the one thing it cannot remove.
    • To record, talk about your day or read a page aloud.
    • A file can be WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC, OGG or WebM, up to 30 MB.
